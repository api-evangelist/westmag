# Western Magnetics (Westmag)

> Western Magnetics ("Westmag") designs and manufactures BLDC drone motors and
> robot actuators in South San Francisco, California — built and scaled in
> America to serve the global drone and robotics sector. NDAA-compliant from
> day one, with materials and components sourced from the US and allied nations.

Westmag is building the great American motor company. Motors and actuators —
which have mostly been manufactured overseas — are what make drones and robots
move. Westmag is bringing that production back to the US with a vertically
integrated platform spanning design, manufacturing, and supply chain. A drone
motor sits at the heart of every robot actuator, so Westmag builds both; the
shared architecture compounds into better performance and economies of scale.

- **Company:** Western Magnetics Company (Westmag)
- **Headquarters / Factory 01:** South San Francisco, California, USA
- **Contact:** contact@westmag.com
- **Sector:** Brushless DC (BLDC) motors and actuators for drones and robotics
- **Differentiators:** US-made, NDAA-compliant, vertically integrated, US + allied-nation sourcing
- **Investors:** Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Founders Fund, Lux Capital, Menlo Ventures, NFDG
- **Leadership:** David Hansen (Co-Founder & CEO), Jordan Sanders (Co-Founder & COO)

## Products

- **Drone Motors:** High-volume production across a range of BLDC motor types
  for Group 1, 2, and 3 UAS platforms. 20–100 mm stators, 5″–50″ props, Kv
  100–3,000, outrunner and inrunner.
- **Robot Actuators:** Integrated actuators for humanoids and mobile robots.
  Torque 1–150 Nm, 24–48 V, gearing 6:1–12:1.
